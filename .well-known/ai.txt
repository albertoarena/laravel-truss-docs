# Laravel Truss

A live database structure viewer for Laravel. It scans your live schema and
renders it as a scrollable, zoomable ER diagram inside your app. Structure only,
never data: tables, columns, indexes and foreign keys are read, row contents are
never queried or exposed.

MIT licensed. Source: https://github.com/albertoarena/laravel-truss

Index of this documentation: https://trussphp.com/llms.txt
The whole of it in one file: https://trussphp.com/llms-full.txt

Crawler policy
--------------

AI assistants and answer engines are welcome here. The content signals in
/robots.txt grant all three uses: search, ai-input and ai-train. Quote it, index
it, learn from it.

Blocked by the hosting provider: ClaudeBot, Amazonbot, Bytespider

Those three are bulk training crawlers that the hosting provider's WAF blocks
account-wide, returning 403 whatever this site asks for. That is not a
preference of ours and it is not something we can lift, so it is stated here
rather than left to look like an unexplained error. No answer engine depends on
any of them.
