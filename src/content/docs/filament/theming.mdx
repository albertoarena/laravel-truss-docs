The diagram **reads the panel's own CSS custom properties at runtime**. It follows the panel it is installed in, including one it has never seen, with no configuration, no build step and no palette to keep in step.

There is nothing to do, which is the point of the page.

:::note[Checked rather than reasoned about]
Every claim below was verified in a browser on **17/09/2026**, against Truss v1.13.0, Filament 5.8.1, Laravel 13 and Chrome. The screenshots were taken from the same panel on 18/09/2026.
:::

## What follows the panel

**The primary colour.** With `->colors(['primary' => Color::Teal])`, the rule under the toolbar, the focused table's border, the focus ring, the checkbox accent, the zoom control and its slider, the legend keys, a utility button's open state and the diff button all go teal. Nothing is left on the default amber.

**The grey scale.** `->colors(['gray' => Color::Slate])` moves the diagram background, the entity fill, the hairlines, the blueprint grid and the entity text with it.

**Dark mode**, instantly and with no reload, because the panel's own toggle is what the diagram listens to.

**The typeface.** `->font('Poppins')` takes the heading, the toolbar labels, the inputs and the footer.

**The layout.** Top navigation, full content width and a collapsing sidebar were each run: the diagram box ends where the page ends and nothing overflows sideways. Its height is measured from the page rather than assumed, so a panel with a taller heading or a breadcrumb gets the right answer.

**A custom compiled theme**, from `php artisan make:filament-theme` and `viteTheme()`, which replaces Filament's own stylesheet entirely. Everything above still holds.

## What does not follow, deliberately

**The diagram itself stays monospaced**, and so do the legend's `PK` and `FK` keys, whatever the panel's font is. Those are notation rather than interface, and a schema read in a proportional face is harder to scan. It is a line rather than an oversight.

**Severity colours stay severe.** Warnings and errors take Filament's `--warning-*` and `--danger-*` rather than the primary, so a red health badge stays red on a teal panel. A severity is not an accent.

**`PK` and `FK` badges** are entity text and take the grey scale, not the primary.

## The one caveat

**Colours come from `->colors()`, not from the theme's own CSS.**

Overriding Tailwind's `--color-gray-*` inside `resources/css/filament/<panel>/theme.css` changes nothing here. That is not a gap in the plugin: Filament emits its `--gray-*` custom properties from `colors()`, so a Tailwind-level override moves neither the panel's own chrome nor the diagram. Both ways were measured.

The rule worth remembering: **whatever changes Filament's palette changes the diagram, and whatever does not, does not.**

## There is no theming config here

The plugin adds no theming setting, and **`truss.theme` does not reach this page**. That config colours [Truss's own dashboard](/guides/theming/) at `/truss`, and it is read on the server, while a Filament panel decides its theme in the browser. The separation is deliberate rather than pending.

What is promised is that the page reads the panel's properties, so a theme nobody anticipated arrives with the right values. Whether a particular grey step reads well at a particular size is a judgement, and that is the one thing here nobody has automated.
